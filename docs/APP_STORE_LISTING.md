### Description (1926/4000)
Engine Eye helps you make sense of car trouble.

Describe what's going wrong, or take a photo of a warning light, a part, or a mechanic's estimate, and Engine Eye gives you a clear explanation in plain English: how urgent it is, the most likely causes, simple checks you can do yourself, and rough DIY and shop cost ranges.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Diagnose a problem: type what you're seeing, hearing, or feeling, or add a photo. Ask follow-up questions to dig deeper.
• Understand a repair quote: photograph an estimate and see which items look necessary, which can wait, and which look overpriced.
• Look up OBD-II codes: enter a code such as P0420 for what it means, common causes, and what to do next.
• Check for safety recalls: Engine Eye looks up open recalls for your vehicle from the U.S. government's NHTSA database. Hide the list if you'd rather not see it.
• Find repair videos: get suggested how-to videos matched to your problem.
• Keep track of your car: log repairs, record your mileage, and set maintenance reminders so nothing gets forgotten.
• Find a shop: jump to nearby auto repair shops in Maps.
• Add your vehicles (optional): it makes answers more specific, but you can use the app without one.
• Sign in with Face ID after your first sign-in.

YOUR PRIVACY AND CONTROL
Engine Eye asks your permission before your questions and photos are sent to its AI service. You can withdraw that permission at any time in Settings, and you can delete your account and saved diagnoses from inside the app.

IMPORTANT
Engine Eye provides general information generated by artificial intelligence. It is not a substitute for a professional inspection, and it can be wrong. Always check with a qualified mechanic before repairs, especially for brakes, steering, fuel, or electrical problems. If you think your car is unsafe to drive, don't drive it.

Questions or feedback? Contact us from the Help & FAQ screen in the app.

Engine Eye Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Engine Eye ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use the Engine Eye mobile application. By using Engine Eye, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

  • Email address and encrypted password when you register
  • Account creation date and last login timestamp

Vehicle Information

  • Vehicle year, make, model, engine size, and mileage you provide
  • Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) if provided for recall lookup
  • Maintenance history and repair logs you create

Diagnostic Data

  • Text descriptions of car problems you submit
  • Photos and images you upload for diagnosis
  • OBD-II diagnostic trouble codes you enter
  • Mechanic quotes you upload for analysis

Usage Data

  • We do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking tools in the App
  • If you choose to share app analytics with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash and performance reports

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide AI-powered vehicle diagnostics through Anthropic's Claude AI
  • Analyze mechanic quotes and provide cost comparisons
  • Look up vehicle-specific recall data from the NHTSA database
  • Decode VIN numbers to auto-populate vehicle information
  • Save and display your diagnosis and repair history
  • Track maintenance schedules across multiple vehicles
  • Suggest relevant DIY repair videos via YouTube
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • Keep the App working reliably
  • Respond to your support requests and inquiries
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. How We Store Your Information

Your data is stored in the following ways:

Local Device Storage

Vehicle profiles, repair history, maintenance schedules, mileage, and saved quote analyses are stored on your device using encrypted secure storage. This data remains on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

Secure Cloud Storage

Your account (email and encrypted password) and your saved diagnoses, including their conversations, are stored securely with our database provider, Supabase. Passwords are hashed and salted — we never store your plain text password. Access rules ensure each user can read and change only their own saved diagnoses.

Diagnostic Processing

When you submit a diagnosis, your query and any images are transmitted securely to our backend server and then to Anthropic's Claude AI for processing. Our backend server does not keep your conversations. They are saved to your account history, which is stored with our database provider (Supabase), so you can reopen them. They stay there until you delete them or delete your account.

4. Third-Party Services

Engine Eye uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

Anthropic (Claude AI)

Powers our AI diagnostic engine. Your diagnostic queries and images are sent to Anthropic for processing. Anthropic's privacy policy governs their handling of this data. See anthropic.com/privacy.

NHTSA (National Highway Traffic Safety Administration)

A U.S. government public API used to retrieve vehicle recall data and decode VIN numbers. No personal data is shared with NHTSA beyond your vehicle's year, make, model, or VIN.

YouTube Data API

Used to suggest relevant DIY repair videos based on your diagnosed issue. Search queries related to your diagnosis may be sent to YouTube. Google's privacy policy applies. See policies.google.com/privacy.

Supabase

Provides secure user authentication and database services. Your email and encrypted credentials are stored on Supabase's servers. See supabase.com/privacy.

Railway

Hosts our backend API server. Diagnostic requests pass through Railway's infrastructure. See railway.app/legal/privacy.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With third-party service providers listed above, solely to operate the App
  • If required by law, court order, or government authority
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of Engine Eye or its users
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with prior notice to you
  • With your explicit consent for any other purpose

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between the App and our servers
  • Encrypted local storage on your device using Expo SecureStore
  • Bcrypt password hashing — your password is never stored in plain text
  • Secure authentication tokens with expiration
  • Access rules on our database so each user can only reach their own saved diagnoses

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Right to Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of any non-essential data processing
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent

You can delete your account and all of your saved diagnoses yourself at any time in the app under Settings, Delete Account. You can also withdraw your permission to send information to our AI service under Settings, Withdraw AI permission.

For any other request, contact us at jack.evans4408@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete your personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights. Engine Eye does not sell personal information. To submit a CCPA request, contact us at jack.evans4408@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete it. 10. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. Local device data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the App. Account data is retained until you request deletion. If you request account deletion, we will delete your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Engine Eye is a mobile application and does not use browser cookies. We do not use analytics or advertising tools in the App. We do not use cross-app tracking or share your device advertising ID with third parties for advertising purposes.
